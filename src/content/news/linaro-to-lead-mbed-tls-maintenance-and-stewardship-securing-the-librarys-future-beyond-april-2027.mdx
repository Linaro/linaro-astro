Linaro today announced that it will take on maintenance and stewardship of Mbed TLS, the widely adopted open-source library implementing TLS and X.509 standards, ensuring its continued development and support beyond April 2027.

The transition brings together Linaro, NXP and Espressif, with all three organisations committing engineering resources to support the project's future. The initiative will operate under the existing TrustedFirmware.org governance framework, preserving the project's open and vendor-neutral development model.

The transition follows a community-wide effort to identify a sustainable maintenance model for Mbed TLS. Nearly 20 organisations expressed interest in supporting the project, demonstrating the importance of the library to the embedded and connected device ecosystem.

# **A collaborative commitment to long-term maintenance**

As part of the transition, Linaro will lead the maintenance effort, with engineers from NXP and Espressif working alongside the current Mbed TLS project team ahead of the planned April 2027 handover.

Linaro will take responsibility for maintenance of the Mbed TLS 4.x branches, with an initial focus on security fixes, releases and maintaining the health of the 4.1 Long-Term Support (LTS) code base.

The baseline maintenance commitment will prioritise monitoring and handling high-severity vulnerabilities in Mbed TLS 4.1 LTS, with maintenance aligned to the lifetime of the release.

The transition applies specifically to the Mbed TLS library. TF-PSA-Crypto will continue to be maintained by the existing Mbed TLS project team.

# **Preserving open development and governance**

Mbed TLS will remain under TrustedFirmware.org governance, ensuring continuity of its open development model, licensing and community participation.

Linaro brings significant experience maintaining Mbed TLS, as well as a long history of managing collaborative engineering initiatives across the semiconductor and software industries.

Its existing involvement in the Trusted Firmware ecosystem, including the maintenance of Trusted Firmware dashboards and operation of the Trusted Firmware hardware lab, provides additional engineering infrastructure to support the project.

# **Beyond baseline maintenance: supporting product requirements**

In addition to the baseline maintenance commitment, Linaro will offer extended engineering and maintenance services for organisations requiring broader support.

These services can include:

* **Extended maintenance:** Fixes across all vulnerability severities, support for additional branches and extended maintenance periods.
* **Customisation and platform support:** Platform ports, hardware acceleration and product-specific changes maintained across releases.
* **Migration and feature development:** Support for migration to Mbed TLS 4.1 LTS, development of additional features and prioritised upstream contributions.
* **Integration services:** Integration and optimisation within platforms including Zephyr, Trusted Firmware, RTOS environments and Linux-based systems.
* **Compliance support:** Vulnerability handling records and Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) inputs to support manufacturers' own Cyber Resilience Act processes.
* **Dedicated engineering support:** Direct collaboration with Linaro engineers to address specific product and platform requirements.

These additional services are designed to help organisations maintain control over their product security, software lifecycle and platform-specific requirements.

# **An open invitation to the community**

The transition represents a collaborative commitment to the long-term sustainability of Mbed TLS. Linaro welcomes further participation from organisations and individual contributors who depend on the library.

Companies interested in contributing engineering resources, learning more about the maintenance model or discussing dedicated support can contact Linaro.

The handover preparation period will run through April 2027, with engineers from Linaro, NXP and Espressif working alongside the existing project team to ensure a smooth transfer of knowledge and responsibilities.
